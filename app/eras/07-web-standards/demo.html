Your boss says tables are how websites are built. Your boss also prints out his email. This page has no layout tables, no <font> tags and no spacer GIFs. Everything you see is decided by one external stylesheet.

01 Why plant in CSS?

Structure goes in the markup; presentation goes in the stylesheet. Keep them apart and a redesign becomes a single file upload, your pages shrink by a third, and screen readers finally hear headings instead of nested <td> soup.

“Markup is a promise about meaning. Style is a suggestion about looks.”

overheard at a web standards meetup, 2004

02 Pruning the markup

  • Headings are <h1> to <h3>, in order. No more <b><font size="4">.
  • Navigation is a list, because it is a list.
  • Lowercase tags, quoted attributes, every element closed. <br /> included.
  • The logo is real text, swapped for a picture with text-indent.

Hacks every gardener knows by heart

The box model hack
Internet Explorer 5 puts padding inside the width. Feed it one width, then hide the right one behind a parser bug.
The clearfix
Floats escape their parent. A generated :after block with clear: both pulls the parent back around them.
The double-margin float bug
IE6 doubles the margin on a floated box. Add display: inline; nobody else minds.

03 Submit a design
