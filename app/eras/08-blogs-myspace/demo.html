I made my kettle send me an email (sort of)

So everyone at work is saying “Ajax” now like it's a new kind of soap. I spent Sunday wiring the old webcam above the kettle to a page that refreshes itself without reloading. It asks the server “is it boiling yet?” every five seconds. The answer is usually no.

Next step: an RSS feed for the kettle. Subscribe in your aggregator and you'll never miss a brew again. Yes, I know. Yes, I know.
