Help & FAQ

Q: Why can't I bookmark this page?
A: Frames keep one address for the whole frameset. Bookmark the frames page and use the menu.

Q: The page opened inside itself!
A: Someone forgot target="_top". Happens to the best of us.

Outbound links were written as <a href="..." target="_top"> so the other site replaced the whole frameset instead of loading inside our main frame.
